The problem

What it is there to fix.

Movement sits outside the platform

Fleet, goods and labour coordination run on separate tools that share nothing with the operational systems around them.

Roles need different views

A driver, a dispatcher, a labour supervisor and a customer each need a different surface onto the same job.

Proof of movement is weak

Where a vehicle went and when a job completed is reconstructed from phone calls rather than recorded as it happens.

How it works

From input to outcome.

  1. RequestA movement job is raised with its route and load
  2. AssignVehicle, driver and labour matched to the job
  3. TrackPosition and status update as the job runs
  4. ConfirmCompletion captured with time and location
  5. ReportUtilisation, timing and exception reporting
