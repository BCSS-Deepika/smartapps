Why SmartCOP

Policing on the beat needs many systems, all at once.

Minutes decide outcomes

A 112 call, an SOS or a cyber fraud in its golden hour needs the nearest officer to act straight away.

Too many separate tools

Record searches, cameras, challans, evidence and duty records each come from a different system.

Every step must hold up

Evidence, searches and visits must be recorded properly so they stand in court and in audit.

SmartCOP brings it together: respond, prevent, record.

Read it downward

A request starts at the officer, passes through shared services, reaches the state systems and the data layer, and returns as an action on the same screen.

Layers 01–05

The request path. Each layer talks only to the one below it, so a module can change without touching an integration.

Layers 06–07 Security, governance and infrastructure are not a step in the path. They apply to every layer above, on every transaction.
