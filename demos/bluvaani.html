Why BluVaani

A fraud victim calls once.
The system must not lose a word.

Details get lost on the call

A frightened caller explains a fraud in pieces. The amount, payment method, reference number and time are easy to miss when they are noted by hand.

Victims don't hear back

After the call, the citizen often has no confirmation that a complaint exists, or what to send next.

Leaders can't see the pipeline

Without one record per complaint, nobody can see what is pending with officers, waiting for NCRP filing, or rejected and needing correction.

BluVaani joins it up: answer, understand, act.

Safeguards

Fast for the citizen.
Careful for the department.

A person is always one step away

Unclear audio, AI errors and complex calls go to officer desks 1001, 1003, 1004 and 1009. The AI line never leaves a citizen stuck.

A strict messaging rule

Automated financial-fraud messages go out only when fraud is confirmed, the category is fraud with loss, and money actually left the account.

Numbers are masked

Phone numbers and transaction references are masked on screen, for example +91 98•• ••41 and UPI-TXN-••4201.

One record per complaint

Every call is linked to its case. Follow-up calls and duplicate reports join the existing case instead of creating new ones.

Honest labels

Every component is marked ESTABLISHED, SIMULATED or ILLUSTRATIVE, so nobody mistakes a demo adapter for a live integration.

Runs fully local

The demo runs offline with an in-repo simulation. It is not connected to live NCRP, WhatsApp or PBX systems.
