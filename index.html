Platform 01 · Access Genie

From passive surveillance to proactive intelligence.

Access Genie adds detection, alerting and evidence search to the cameras, recorders and record systems an organisation already runs — with nothing ripped out. The same four steps run behind every module, whether the site is a highway corridor, a jewellery floor or a police station.

  1. 01CollectStreams from IP and analog cameras, ANPR and radar, plus records, helplines and seized devices.
  2. 02DetectModels run on zones, time windows and thresholds the organisation sets — at the edge or in the cluster.
  3. 03AlertA detection becomes a prioritised alert with its evidence attached, routed to whoever owns it.
  4. 04ActThe alert lands in a workflow: a dispatch, a challan, a case, a memo, a floor-team instruction.

Built on existing cameras

IP and analog CCTV, PTZ, city and station cameras keep recording as before. Access Genie reads the streams and adds a layer on top; the recorder and the wiring stay where they are.

Processing stays where you decide

Fully on-premise, private cloud or hybrid. For most government deployments, video, records and evidence are processed and stored on infrastructure the department owns.

Connected, not another silo

Reads from and writes to the systems already in use, so officers and staff do not key the same data in twice. Each connection is scoped, tested and approved before it goes live.

Connected workflows

Follow one case across the portfolio.

The point of a portfolio is what happens between the products. Each workflow below follows one real operational situation through several platforms — and each step is labelled with how mature that connection actually is, so a proposed integration is never presented as a live one.

  1. Blura SAGAA digital signal is identified Public posts, videos, comments and news from configured sources are collected. AI reads language, tone and intent, and the item is scored critical, high, medium or low. Established in product
  2. GARUDAThe finding is reviewed and escalated The alert lands in GARUDA's feed against the subject's GARUDA ID, with the evidence attached and the source, timestamp and hash preserved. Integration level 3 · data exchange
  3. Geo IntelligencePlaced on the map Where the signal has a location, it joins the hotspot, beat and geo-fence layers so a commander sees where to act. Integration level 2 · deep link
  4. SmartCOPThe field officer receives the task A task is pushed to the officer's phone with the alert context. The officer accepts, acts, captures geotagged evidence and updates the status, which returns to GARUDA. Established in product
  1. BluVaaniA citizen calls The call is answered in whichever language the citizen speaks, transcribed with speakers separated, and scored for intent, urgency, place and names. Established in product
  2. BluVaaniRouted and handed off Dispatched to the right station, unit or patrol by jurisdiction and availability. A complaint is handed to the documentation products with the transcript attached. Established in product
  3. AI CopWriterStructured into a draft The complaint is translated and placed into the report's five Ws and one H. Missing elements are flagged rather than left silently blank. Handoff defined, per-deployment integration
  4. FIR AutomationVerified and registered Statutory sections are suggested with reasons; the station house officer checks, edits and approves, and only then is it registered in the crime records system. Proposed · validate the records interface per state
A human approves before registration. Section suggestions are decision support. Nothing is registered on the AI's own output.
  1. SAGA / BluVaaniA grievance is received From public conversation detected by Blura SAGA, from a petition at the desk, or from a citizen call handled by BluVaani. Established in product
  2. Grievance ManagementClassified and routed Sorted into complaint, criticism, suggestion or query, assigned a category and station, and routed with a response-time timer. Established in product
  3. Grievance / SmartCOPThe responsible desk acts Remarks are required at every step. Where field action is needed, the task reaches the officer on SmartCOP. Integration level 2 · deep link
  4. GARUDAResolution and closure tracked The citizen is updated, closure is recorded, and grievance trends by station and category feed GARUDA's dashboard. Integration level 3 · data exchange
  1. Store camerasFeeds are analysed Streams are decoded at the edge or in the cluster, and models run against the zones, time windows and thresholds the chain has set. Established in product
  2. Correlation engineEvents are identified A detection becomes a sales, risk or operations event against one of the sixteen KPIs, correlated over time rather than fired per frame. Established in product
  3. DispatchEvidence and alerts are generated Local siren and strobe where the event warrants it, plus an encrypted alert payload to messaging, mail and the live dashboard. Every clip and snapshot is hash-stamped. Established in product
  4. LeadershipPerformance reviewed across stores A nightly executive scorecard delivers store-wise KPIs, violation logs and conversion metrics, so the picture does not depend on anyone compiling it. Established in product
How to read the labels. Established means the capability is built and working in that product today. Integration level / defined means the connection pattern is specified and implemented per deployment. Proposed means it has not yet been validated against a production system — each connection is scoped, tested and approved before it goes live.
Technology foundations

Where it runs is your decision.

The same platforms run where the organisation decides. Most government deployments keep data inside the department: video, records and evidence are processed and stored on infrastructure the department owns, and nothing is sent to outside AI services.

Option 01

Fully on-premise

Everything runs on servers inside the organisation's premises. Video, records and evidence never leave. Language and AI models run on local GPU servers.

Option 02

Private cloud

A dedicated cluster the organisation controls, with elastic compute for peak load. Suited to estates with many sites and variable inference demand.

Option 03

Hybrid

Inference at the edge with central correlation and reporting. Only metadata, vectors and alert snapshots cross the network — not video.

Security and sovereignty

  • Sovereign processing — AI runs on servers inside the organisation's premises where the deployment requires it.
  • Zero-trust access — identity-based, multi-factor, least privilege, micro-segmented.
  • Encryption — at rest and in transit, with key management under the organisation's control.
  • Role-based access — what a person sees depends on their rank, unit and jurisdiction; access is removed in one click on transfer.
  • Immutable audit — every search, view, download, approval and AI draft recorded with who, when and why, exportable for audit or court.
  • Sensitive data masked — identifiers and victim details masked by default; revealing them needs a reason, and is logged.

Evidence that holds up

  • Hashed on capture — evidence is hashed at the moment of capture, before upload.
  • Chain of custody — every step from capture to report is logged, with statutory certificates produced for digital records.
  • Geotag and timestamp — location and time watermarked onto field evidence.
  • Provenance travels with the finding — source, timestamp and hash stay attached, because an AI assessment is not admissible alone.
  • AI suggests, a human decides — face matches, risk scores, section suggestions and drafted documents are reviewed and approved before any action.
  • Forensic soundness — seized-device data is imaged write-protected with the original untouched.
