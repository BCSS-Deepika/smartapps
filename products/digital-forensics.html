The problem

What it is there to fix.

Evidence integrity is fragile

A device examined without write protection can be challenged in court, and the case can turn on that alone.

Extraction is only the start

Thousands of chats, calls and media files mean nothing until someone can search, link and date them against the case.

Manipulated media is now routine

Audio and video submitted as evidence may be edited or synthetic, and that has to be tested rather than assumed.

How it works

From input to outcome.

  1. ImageDevice copied with a hash; original untouched
  2. ExtractContacts, chats, calls, photos, locations, payment apps
  3. AnalyseKeyword, face and link search; tamper and manipulation checks
  4. LinkFindings attached to the case and GARUDA ID
  5. CertifyReport with chain of custody and statutory certificate

Who uses it

Forensic labsCyber crimeInvestigating officers
