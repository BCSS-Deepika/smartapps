Why BluFlow

Organisations are not short of financial data.
They have too much of it, in too many places.

Finance runs several bank accounts. The property team keeps leases in its own system. Procurement holds the tenders, legal holds the contracts, and project teams track budgets on their own. Each system works. The trouble starts when a question cuts across all of them.

The records live apart

Accounts, vendors, assets, contracts and projects sit in different systems, owned by different teams, in formats never meant to talk to each other.

The questions cross them

Where did the money come from? What was it spent on? What should have been received? What is still outstanding? One payment can touch five records.

Answers are stitched by hand

Someone chases five people across five systems and rebuilds the story. It is slow, and the answer is only as good as the last person who remembered where a file was.

BluFlow does the stitching: collect, connect, understand, act.
From records to understanding

Each record tells part of the story.
Together they explain the money.

Any one of these records says something. Read alone, each is a fragment. Click through them to watch the picture build: the account, the transaction, the contract and the asset stop being separate facts and start telling one story.

How BluFlow works

Six steps.
From a pile of statements to a report someone can sign.

Each step is modest on its own; the value comes from doing all of them in one place. A statement parser stops after extraction. A dashboard stops after visualisation. BluFlow carries on into context and intelligence.

Operating model · nine stages in detail
  1. Bring in data from authorised financial and organisational sources.
  2. Extract information from statements, documents and structured files.
  3. Normalise dates, amounts, identifiers, transaction types and other financial fields.
  4. Resolve or associate entities where appropriate.
  5. Connect financial records with accounts, projects, assets, vendors, contracts and other context.
  6. Provide dashboards, search, timelines and relationship views.
  7. Apply rules, analytics and AI to surface trends, exceptions and relationships.
  8. Allow deeper investigation when a user needs to explain a pattern or trace a flow.
  9. Generate structured outputs for management, audit, investigation or review.

AI inside BluFlow

AI reduces the work of understanding.
It never hides the evidence.

AI is most useful when it shortens the path through large volumes of financial records, while every answer stays linked to the record it came from.

The rule for trust

AI can help explain the records. The record remains the evidence. Authorised professionals remain responsible for formal conclusions.

  • An AI explanation is a working summary, not a finding.
  • Source facts, analytical signals and human conclusions are kept separate.
  • BluFlow never declares a transaction fraudulent or criminal on its own.
  • Reports keep links back to the underlying records.
BluFlow and existing systems

An intelligence layer above your systems.
Not a rip-and-replace.

Keep the ERP, banking, procurement, property and legal systems for the jobs they do. BluFlow makes the information they hold useful when it has to be understood across system boundaries.
