Why it is needed

Writing an FIR by hand is slow, and key details get missed.

Language gap

Complainants explain what happened in their own language. The FIR is written in another, and meaning gets lost in between.

Time at the desk

The officer writes, re-reads and rewrites the statement by hand while the complainant waits.

Missing details

If the place, time, accused or motive is not recorded, the case is weaker from the first day.

AI-CopWriter handles all three: record, understand, check.

What the station gets

Everything needed to record, review and keep complaints.

Live transcription

Text appears on screen while the complainant is still speaking.

Petition scanning

Read petitions from PDF or photos (PNG, JPG, TIFF and more) into editable text.

Speaker separation

Shows who said what when the officer and complainant both speak.

FIR quality audit

A 12-point report marks each 5W + 1H element present, partial or missing, and works out any delay in reporting.

Searchable records

Every complaint is saved with its original audio, ready to search, replay and edit.

Secure access

Personal logins for each officer, an admin panel, and records stored on your own server.

AI-CopWriter is available in the browser for the station desk, and as an Android and iOS app for officers in the field.
