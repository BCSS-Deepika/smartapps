Integration Topology

Three deployment modes connecting
store cameras to Access Genie.

Designed to adapt seamlessly across flagship megastores, suburban boutique showrooms and regional shopping mall formats.

MODE 1 · EDGE

On-Premise Edge Gateways

Heavy retail stores with high camera density run compact NVIDIA Jetson edge inference nodes. RTSP streams are decoded locally with zero WAN video streaming, sending only JSON metadata, face vectors and alert snapshots to the central cloud.

Store cameras→Jetson gateway→JSON + snapshots→Cloud
BANDWIDTH: <50 KBPS PER STORE · ULTRA LOW LATENCY
MODE 2 · CLOUD

Centralized Cloud Ingestion Cluster

Showrooms connected via high-speed SD-WAN stream direct RTSP/WebRTC feeds to the chain’s private cloud compute cluster. Elastic GPU workers scale inference during morning store openings and evening peak shopping traffic.

Store cameras→SD-WAN→Elastic GPU cluster
CENTRALIZED GPU CLUSTER · ZERO LOCAL HARDWARE
MODE 3 · HYBRID DISPATCH

Hybrid Multi-Channel Dispatch

Edge inferences trigger local sirens and strobes while asynchronously dispatching encrypted alert payloads to the WhatsApp Business API, SMTP Mail Relay and the Live Surveillance WebSockets Dashboard in under 500 milliseconds.

Edge alert→SirenWhatsAppEmailDashboard
WHATSAPP · EMAIL · WEBSOCKETS · SMS ESCALATION
Enterprise Safeguards & Governance

Accountability for the management.
Fast for the floor team.

Access Genie combines automated computer vision with enterprise-grade security, evidence cryptography and multi-tenant administrative governance.

Super Admin Portal

Single pane of glass for adding new stores, registering camera RTSP streams, assigning role-based permissions (CSO, Floor Manager, Hospitality Lead) and adjusting KPI alert thresholds.

SHA-256 Evidence Integrity

Every alert video clip and high-resolution snapshot is cryptographically stamped with SHA-256 hashes and UTC timestamps, preventing tampering and ensuring legal admissibility.

Camera Heartbeat SLA

Continuous polling across 1,000+ CCTV endpoints ensures a 99.9% camera uptime SLA. Automated escalations alert technicians before blind spots can be exploited.

Daily WhatsApp Summaries

Automated executive scorecard generated every night at 22:30 IST. Delivers store-wise KPI scorecards, violation logs and conversion metrics directly to leadership phones.
