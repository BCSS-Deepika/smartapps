Why One Ecosystem

Policing needs many systems.
Officers need one picture.

Too many separate tools

Social media, drug records, bank statements, devices and paperwork each sit in a different system with a different login.

Intelligence stays in silos

What one unit finds about a person is not seen by the station or wing working the same person next door.

Every step must hold up

Evidence, searches and decisions must be recorded properly so they stand in court, in audit and in RTI.

Safeguards

Fast for the officer.
Accountable for the department.

The same rules apply in every product, because every product goes through the same front door.

One login, role-based access

Single sign-on across all products. What an officer sees depends on their rank, unit and jurisdiction. Access is removed in one click on transfer.

Every action logged

Searches, views, downloads, approvals and AI drafts are recorded with who, when and why. The audit trail can be exported for vigilance, RTI or court.

AI suggests, the officer decides

FIR sections, risk scores, face matches and drafted documents are suggestions. An authorised officer reviews and approves each one before any action.

Data stays in the state

Hosted in the state data centre. Language and AI models run on local GPU servers, as Blura SAGA's on-premise deployment already does, so case data is not sent to outside AI services.

Sensitive data masked

Phone numbers, account numbers, dates of birth and victim details are masked by default. Revealing them needs a reason, and the reveal is logged.

Evidence that holds in court

Evidence is hashed when captured and its chain of custody is recorded. Section 63 BSA certificates are produced for digital records.
