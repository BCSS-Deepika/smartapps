Access Genie

From passive surveillance to proactive intelligence.

AI video and operational intelligence. Access Genie adds detection, alerting and evidence search to the cameras, recorders and record systems an organisation already runs — with nothing ripped out.

Built on existing cameras

IP and analog CCTV, PTZ, city and station cameras keep recording as before. Access Genie reads the streams and adds a layer on top; the recorder and the wiring stay where they are.

Processing stays where you decide

Fully on-premise, private cloud or hybrid. For most government deployments, video, records and evidence are processed and stored on infrastructure the department owns.

Connected, not another silo

Reads from and writes to the systems already in use, so officers and staff do not key the same data in twice. Each connection is scoped, tested and approved before it goes live.

One platform, two divisions

The core modules and hardware run on the cameras an organisation already has. Above them sit two divisions: Policing & public safety, and Enterprise & industry.

Core platformThe intelligence layer on every camera3

Watches live and recorded video, recognises the events an organisation defines and raises an alert with the evidence attached.

Watch every feed
Every stream is analysed around the clock, so operators review alerts instead of staring at screens.
What it can detect
Identity: face recognition, watchlist match, person search across cameras. Public order: crowd gathering, overcrowding, loitering, night movement. Threats: intrusion, restricted zones, gate jumping, unattended baggage. Stations: frisking checks, guard absence, after-hours access, camera health.
Investigator search
Face search from one reference photo, attribute search by clothing when no photo exists, a movement timeline across cameras, and watchlist alerts the moment a listed face or plate appears.
Every alert is closed
Assign, acknowledge, resolve or mark a false positive to improve tuning, then report trends by camera, zone and alert type.
Where it runs
On existing IP and PTZ cameras, on new edge AI cameras, or on a BluEdge server inside the premises.
Safeguard
Face recognition is used within the legal authorisation and procedures the organisation sets.

Every vehicle that passes a camera is read, logged and checked against the rules an organisation sets, day and night.

What is captured
Number plate (reflective or not) from multiple angles, time stamp and direction of travel, and a vehicle image with every read, at speed and at night.
Checked against
Watchlists of suspect vehicles, permitted-hours rules for entry and exit, and vehicle registration records.
Rules
Watchlist hit → instant alert. Time-rule breach → ticket alert. Partly hidden, tampered or cloned plate → flag for review. Loitering, erratic movement or repeat crossings → pattern alert.
How an alert reaches the team
Plate read → rule check → ticket raised with images → SMS, email and messaging to the officers.

A browser-based VMS that brings IP and analog cameras, DVRs and NVRs into one console for monitoring, recording, investigation and reporting, on-premises or in the cloud.

Unified monitoring
Video wall and custom layouts, live PTZ and digital zoom, two-way audio, a browser console for the command centre.
Recording & playback
Continuous, motion and event recording with pre- and post-event buffers, synchronised multi-camera playback, smart search, bookmarks and clip export.
Forensic search
People, vehicle and object tracking, face search from a photo, attribute search and a cross-camera timeline.
Alerts, maps & health
A central alert centre, cameras pinned on site and floor plans, and camera, recorder and storage health with scheduled reports.
Device management
ONVIF discovery and onboarding, bulk import, firmware upgrades and remote reboot. Existing recordings stay usable.

The on-premises AI server that runs Access Genie inside the organisation's premises, so sensitive data never has to leave.

Built in
Sovereign processing, zero-trust access with MFA and least privilege, encryption at rest and in transit, continuous threat detection and high availability.
What it runs
Video analytics and VMS, LLM and AI workloads, investigation platforms, dashboards and analytics, and internal communication.
Architecture
Enterprise storage chassis with room for GPU configurations, expandable high-density storage, open and Kubernetes-ready.

The devices that capture what Access Genie analyses, on roads, bridges and checkpoints.

Edge AI camera
5 MP camera with AI on board for ANPR, loitering, face recognition and people counting; IR night vision to 30 m, IP66.
4K global-shutter ANPR camera
Captures fast traffic with no motion blur across 3–4 lanes; one camera handles both ANPR and violation detection.
Pulsed IR flash & 4D radar
The flash keeps seatbelts, phones and plates readable at night; the radar measures spot and average speed, lane by lane, up to 240 km/h.
AI-VPU edge unit
Runs several violation models at the roadside with up to 1 TB local storage, sending only results over wired or 4G links.

AI-powered traffic surveillance and automated enforcement, from the violation on the road to the challan.

Violations detected
Over-speeding, no helmet, no seatbelt, triple riding, mobile use while driving, wrong way or no entry, signal jumping, wrong parking, improper or obstructed plates, stalled vehicles, and vehicle counts by type.
Seven automated steps
Detect → capture → read the plate → generate evidence → package the record → generate the challan → push to the challan system.
Where it is deployed
Highways and expressways, urban junctions, accident-prone locations, city entry and exit points, restricted zones and school safety zones.
Two ways to deploy
AI added to existing city CCTV — the fastest, lowest-cost route — or a dedicated corridor kit of 4K ANPR cameras, pulsed IR flash, 4D radar and AI-VPU units.
Court-ready evidence
At least two images are saved with every violation, and pulsed IR keeps plates, faces and seatbelts readable at night.

Safeguards

What it will not do, and what it always keeps.

  • Sovereign processing: AI can run on servers inside the organisation's premises.
  • Zero-trust access: identity-based, multi-factor, least privilege, micro-segmented.
  • Encryption at rest and in transit, with key management under the organisation's control.
  • Every alert carries its evidence; every action is logged for audit.
  • Face recognition only within the legal authorisation the organisation sets.
