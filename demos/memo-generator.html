COMPLIANT MEMO GENERATOR

Last night's raids.
This morning's memos.

When a Commissioner's task force raids a gaming den, a hookah centre or a PITA case inside a station's limits, someone on that station failed to act. The Compliant Memo Generator reads the task-force Daily Situation Report, works out which station, sector and officer was responsible, drafts the memo and puts it in front of the Commissioner for approval. The same day, not weeks later.

Why It Matters

Supervision only works
if the memo reaches the right desk, fast.

Drafting took days

Each DSR was read by hand, every case matched to a station, and each memo typed from scratch, while the lapse went cold.

NOW · DRAFTS READY WITHIN MINUTES OF UPLOAD

Who was responsible?

Stations, sectors and transfers change. Working out which SHO or sector SI held that ground on that date was slow and error-prone.

NOW · RESOLVED FROM JURISDICTION + HRMS

Repeat lapses went unseen

Nobody could easily see that the same officer had already been warned twice this cycle, so repeat lapses never escalated.

NOW · 3 WARNINGS RAISE A CHARGE MEMO

The Right Officer

Every case walks down
the hierarchy to one desk.

A city police force is organised Zone → Division → Circle → Police Station → Sector. The generator resolves each case through that chain and picks the officers who held that ground, using officer records mapped from HRMS.

ON HOLD

The Commissioner parks a memo for more facts. It returns to review when ready.

The lapse is not upheld. No memo is issued and it does not count towards escalation.

Only approved warnings count

A warning counts once the Commissioner approves it. Held or rejected memos do not.

The fourth lapse becomes a charge memo

After three approved warnings, the generator offers a charge memo for the officer instead of another warning.

The count resets each cycle

Once a charge memo is raised, the warning count starts again for the next cycle.

Platform Architecture

Five layers,
one clear path.

Connects to GARUDA

Opens from GARUDA's app launcher with the same login. Sends lapse counts by station and officer to GARUDA's dashboard.

Officer master

Officer records mapped from HRMS: rank, posting, station and sector role.

Scheduled checks

An hourly job checks for pending items and escalation conditions.

Safeguards

Fast for the office.
Fair to the officer.

A person approves every memo

The generator drafts; only the Commissioner issues. Wording can be changed before approval.

Every memo traces to a case

Each memo links back to the DSR and the exact case it came from.

Role-based access

Officers see only their own memos. Review and administration are limited to those roles.

The officer is heard

Every memo has a compliance step, and the reply is kept with the memo.
